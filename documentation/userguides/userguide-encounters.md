# Building an Encounter

**Audience:** a GM building a fight at the table with Quick Encounter.

How to get a monster list matched to your party, roll for who noticed whom, and put the result on the map.

Quick Encounter is for building a fight in the moment rather than in prep. Tell it where the party is and how hard you want it, and it proposes monsters from the compendiums you already have installed.

This is GM-only.

## Build the cache first

The first time you open it, click **Refresh cache**. It reads your installed monster compendiums and remembers what is in them, because scanning them every time you open the window would be too slow to be worth using.

The line beside the button tells you how many monsters it currently knows about, or says there is no cache yet. Rebuild it after you install or remove a monster compendium; otherwise leave it alone.

## Pick where and how hard

**Habitat** filters candidates to monsters tagged for that environment -- Arctic, Coastal, Desert, Forest, Grassland and the rest, or Any to skip the filter.

Set the difficulty you are aiming at, and the range of monster CRs you are willing to see. **Variability** decides how much the builder is allowed to stray from a tidy answer, and **Max Recommendations** caps how many options it offers.

If the builder runs out of distinct monster types that fit, it adds more of the ones it already picked rather than quietly widening the CR range on you.

You can force particular monsters in by name, and exclude others the same way, and have the window remember those lists between sessions.

## Roll for who noticed whom

**Detection** decides how the encounter opens, on a five-step ladder:

| Level | What it means |
|---|---|
| Surprised | The party walked into it |
| Outmatched Awareness | The monsters have the better of it |
| Mutual Awareness | Everyone sees everyone |
| Tactical Advantage | The party has the better of it |
| Undetected | The party has not been noticed |

Set it by hand, or click **Roll for Detection** to ask the party for Perception and let their average decide which rung you land on.

## Post it, then deploy it

Posting puts a card in chat: a piece of narration chosen for the habitat **and the time of day**, the detection level written out as prose, and the monsters with their artwork and counts.

**Deploy** places the whole selected group on the canvas in a pattern. That is the bulk tool.

**Or drag one monster** from the card straight onto the map. A drag places exactly one monster where you point, and deliberately ignores the count badge. Foundry handles the drop itself, so permissions, importing from the compendium and grid snapping all behave the way they do from the Actors sidebar.

## Settings worth knowing

- **Quick Encounter enabled** shows or hides the tool. Turning it off removes the toolbar button.
- **Toolbar** chooses which toolbar carries the button. To remove the button entirely, switch the feature off rather than hiding it from both.
- **Odds of Encounter** is the chance a rolled encounter happens at all, with **Encounter Sound** and **No Encounter Sound** playing for each outcome.
- **Chat Card Style** sets the card's theme.

## A note on the cards

Quick Encounter cards still render through Bibliosoph's older card path, so they do not match the styling of injury or critical cards in every detail. They are correct and legible; only the presentation differs. Known issues records it.

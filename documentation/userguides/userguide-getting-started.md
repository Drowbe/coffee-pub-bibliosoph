# Getting Started with Bibliosoph

**Audience:** a GM or player who has just installed Bibliosoph.

The first five minutes: what appears on screen, what each toolbar button does, who may press it, and which guide covers it properly.

Bibliosoph requires Coffee Pub Blacksmith, which must be installed and enabled alongside it.

## What changes the moment you enable it

A Coffee Pub toolbar appears in the scene controls, carrying a button per feature you have switched on. A feature that is off hides its button, so the toolbar only ever shows what you are actually using.

Nothing else changes on its own. No automation runs until you turn it on, and no card is posted until somebody presses something.

| Button | Who | What it does | Guide |
|---|---|---|---|
| Messages | Anyone | Opens the conversation window | [Sending messages](userguide-messages.md) |
| Investigation | Anyone | Searches the area for coins and items | [Searching a room](userguide-investigation.md) |
| Critical Hit | GM | Draws a critical and posts it | [Criticals and fumbles](userguide-outcomes.md) |
| Fumble | GM | Draws a fumble and posts it | [Criticals and fumbles](userguide-outcomes.md) |
| Injuries | GM | Opens the injury picker | [Injuries at the table](userguide-injuries.md) |
| Check-Up | GM | Shows and treats what is afflicting a character | [Injuries at the table](userguide-injuries.md) |
| Inspiration | Anyone | The GM deals a card; a player draws one | [Inspiration cards](userguide-inspiration.md) |
| Quick Encounter | GM | Builds a fight and deploys it | [Building an encounter](userguide-encounters.md) |

## The five minutes

**Say something.** Click Messages, pick the party conversation, type, press ENTER. That is the whole feature working, and it is the fastest way to confirm the module is running.

**Search something.** Click Investigation. You will get a card, and probably nothing else -- until the GM points the five rarity settings at roll tables, there is nothing for it to find. Bibliosoph ships no loot tables on purpose; the treasure at your table is yours.

**Hurt somebody.** As GM, select a token, click Injuries, and click any injury. The wound lands on the token with a burst on the canvas, and the condition and penalties are real -- you will see them on the character sheet.

**Fix them again.** With the same token selected, click Check-Up. Every affliction on that character is listed with a control to treat it.

## Before your first real session

Three decisions are worth making deliberately rather than discovering mid-game:

1. **How much automation you want.** Criticals, fumbles and injuries each have an **Automation** setting running from off, through a toolbar button only, to a prompt when the dice land, to the card posting by itself. Start manual and turn it up.
2. **Whether the GM reads private conversations.** **GM Sees All Conversations** is a table agreement, not a technical detail. Settle it out loud.
3. **Whether players roll to treat injuries.** **Player Treatment Rolls** turns treatment into a Medicine check rather than something the GM does instantly.

Everything configurable is in [settings](userguide-settings.md), by its on-screen name.

## Making the content yours

Injuries, criticals, fumbles and inspiration cards are all journal pages with an editing sheet, not hardcoded behaviour. Bibliosoph ships a starter set of each and expects you to write your own.

Copy a shipped compendium into your world and point the matching **Source** setting at your copy **before** you edit anything -- a compendium that ships with the module is replaced when the module updates. [Writing your own injuries](userguide-authoring-injuries.md) walks through it for injuries, and the other three work the same way.
